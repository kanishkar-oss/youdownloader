Send to YouDownloader

Add this button to your browser's bookmarks bar. On any YouTube video, click it and the video opens in YouDownloader, ready to download. It works in every browser, and you don't need to copy or drag the link.

1. Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar

⇩ Send to YouDownloader ← drag it, don't click it

Don't see a bookmarks bar? Show it first:

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, DiaCtrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac
SafariView → Show Favorites Bar (⌘+Shift+B), then drag the button there
FirefoxView → Toolbars → Bookmarks Toolbar

2. Use it

  1. Open a YouTube video in your browser.
  2. Click Send to YouDownloader in your bookmarks bar.
  3. The first time, your browser asks whether to open YouDownloader. Allow it, and tick "Always allow" if it offers that.
  4. YouDownloader comes to the front with the video loaded. Pick a format and click Download.

If dragging doesn't work

Add a bookmark by hand: right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page / Add bookmark, name it Send to YouDownloader, and paste this as its address (URL):

The button only sends the page's address to YouDownloader on this computer. Nothing goes to any other website.
